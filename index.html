About

A student who ships real work.

I'm a second-year BCA student at Guru Nanak University in Hyderabad. I moved to India in August 2025 to study computer applications, and I've spent that time turning what I learn in class into things people can open and use.

I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python. I care about pages that load quickly, read clearly and work on the cheapest phone in the room. I explain every decision in plain language, so you always know what you're getting.

I also write SEO content and translate between French and English, so your website and your message can work together.

Process

How we'll work together.

Four steps, no surprises. You see progress at every stage.

  1. 01

    Talk

    You tell me what you need. I reply with the scope, timeline and price before any work starts.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    I draft the layout and the wording. You approve it before I write any code.

  3. 03

    Build

    I send you a live preview link, collect your feedback and revise until it's right.

  4. 04

    Launch

    I publish the site, hand over the files and walk you through how to update it.
